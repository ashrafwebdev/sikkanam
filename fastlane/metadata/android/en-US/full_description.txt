Sikkanam (சிக்கனம்) is a simple, private income and expense tracker. The name is Tamil for thrift.

Track what comes in and what goes out:
- Add income or expense entries with an amount, a category, and an optional note
- See your balance, total income, and total expenses at a glance
- Review and delete past entries

Your data stays on your phone. Sikkanam needs no account, shows no ads, and doesn't connect to the internet.

Sikkanam is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license.
